# Weapon Family Review Matrix

## Scope

This matrix is the required review pass for the first rebuild wave:

- bow family
- axe family
- hammer family

## Review Conditions

Every family must be checked for every class in all of these states:

- idle
- walk
- attack
- facing right
- facing left
- world scene
- dungeon scene

## Pass Conditions

A combination passes only if:

- the weapon family is instantly recognizable
- the weapon does not read as an icon or random object
- the grip/hand relationship looks believable enough
- the attack motion reads correctly for the family
- there is no obvious clipping or chest-badge effect
- the silhouette remains readable in dungeon lighting

## Bow Family

### Ranger + Bow

- this is the hero case
- should look exceptional

### Berserker + Bow

- must still clearly read as archery
- must not read like a shield or torso ornament

### Guardian + Bow

- must not clip into the shield side
- must look like a real ranged support case

### Runecaster + Bow

- must not read like a magical staff by mistake
- the bow silhouette must remain obvious

## Axe Family

### Berserker + Axe

- this is the hero case
- should feel powerful and signature

### Ranger + Axe

- must still read as an axe immediately
- should not look too large or blocky

### Guardian + Axe

- must avoid fighting the shield-heavy body
- should feel heavy but controlled

### Runecaster + Axe

- must not look absurdly oversized
- silhouette still needs to read cleanly

## Hammer Family

### Guardian + Hammer

- this is the closest hero-support case
- should feel dense and grounded

### Berserker + Hammer

- must feel like a committed heavy weapon
- should not read as a square or sign

### Ranger + Hammer

- must stay readable at small scale
- should not overpower the body sprite

### Runecaster + Hammer

- must not look like a block on a stick
- attachment must still feel intentional

## Review Outcome Categories

### Excellent

- hero-case quality
- strong class fantasy
- no further action needed

### Good

- readable
- stable
- acceptable for Era 1

### Needs Tuning

- recognizable, but needs offset, scale, or pose refinement

### Rebuild Required

- silhouette unclear
- motion confusing
- or the result feels prototype-level
